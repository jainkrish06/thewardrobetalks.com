# Wardrobe Talks — Administrative Guide

Welcome to your Wardrobe Talks administrative dashboard! This guide is written in plain language to help you manage your luxury couture lookbook website.

---

## 🔐 1. Logging In

1. Open your browser and go to your admin URL:
   `https://admin.thewardrobetalks.com` (locally: `http://localhost:3001`).
2. If you are not logged in, you will be redirected to the **Sign In** screen.
3. Enter your email and password:
   - **Default Credentials**: `admin@wardrobetalks.com` / `wardrobe@2025`
   - *Note: For security, immediately create a new account in the **Admin Users** tab and disable the default login.*
4. Click **Sign In**.

---

## 👗 2. Managing Couture Products

The **Products & Collections** section is where you manage your lookbook designs.

### Adding a New Product:
1. Click the **Add New Design** button in the top right corner.
2. Fill in the fields:
   - **Design Title**: The name of the outfit (e.g., *Royal Silk Anarkali*).
   - **Main Category**: Choose `Women`, `Men`, or `Celebrity`.
   - **Subcategory**: The classification tag (e.g., *Anarkali*, *Bridal Lehenga*).
   - **Fabric & Occasion**: Descriptions (e.g., *Raw Silk*, *Wedding Reception*).
   - **Description & Designer's Note**: Describe the embroidery details.
3. **Upload Photos**:
   - Scroll down to the image upload section.
   - Click **Choose Local File** and select an image from your computer or phone.
   - The file will automatically upload to Cloudinary and appear in the list.
4. **Reorder Photos**:
   - Click **Set Cover** on any photo to make it the primary thumbnail.
   - Use the **Up/Down arrows** to change the order.
   - Click the **Red Trash Icon** to delete a photo.
5. Set the publication status:
   - **Signature Featured**: If checked, this product will appear on the homepage.
   - **Published**: If unchecked, it will remain as a draft and hidden from visitors.
6. Click **Save Design**.

### Duplicating a Product:
If you want to add a similar outfit:
1. Find the product in the list.
2. Click the **Copy Icon** (Duplicate).
3. The editor will open with all details copied. Adjust the title, update images, and click **Save**.

### Deleting a Product:
1. Find the product in the list and click the **Red Trash Icon**.
2. Confirm the deletion in the pop-up box.

---

## 🖼️ 3. Managing Hero Slides (Homepage Slideshow)

1. Navigate to the **Hero Slides** tab.
2. **Add a Slide**:
   - Enter a **Kicker** (small tagline, e.g., *Atelier Collection*).
   - Enter the **Heading** and **Subtitle** to display on top of the slide.
   - Upload the background photo.
   - Enter a **CTA Button Label** (e.g., *Explore Bridal*) and **CTA Target** (e.g., *women*).
   - Click **Create Slide**.
3. **Activate/Deactivate**: Click the **Eye Icon** to show or hide a slide without deleting it.
4. **Reorder**: Use the **Up/Down arrows** to change the slideshow sequence.

---

## 📸 4. Daily Atelier Gallery

Daily Atelier is your Instagram-like gallery.
1. Navigate to the **Daily Atelier** tab.
2. Click **Add Atelier Image** and upload a photo.
3. Enter a caption description and alt text.
4. (Optional) Enter an Instagram URL link so visitors are directed to your post when they click.
5. Click **Save Item**.

---

## ✉️ 5. Handling Client Inquiries

When a visitor submits an enquiry form on the website:
1. Go to the **Inquiries** tab.
2. You will see a list of client submissions with names, contact details, product links, and messages.
3. **Update Status**: Choose from the status drop-down:
   - `NEW` (Received but not reviewed).
   - `CONTACTED` (You have emailed or called them).
   - `IN_PROGRESS` (Consultations active).
   - `CLOSED` (Sale finalized or inquiry resolved).
4. Click the **Trash Icon** to delete records if they are spam.

---

## ⚙️ 6. Editing Website Coordinates

1. Navigate to the **Website Settings** tab.
2. Update contact details:
   - **WhatsApp Contact**: Your primary contact phone number.
   - **WhatsApp Community**: Invite link for notifications.
   - **Instagram URL**: Instagram page link.
   - **Showroom Address**: Renders in the page footer.
   - **Google Maps Pin**: URL link to open in maps.
3. Update brand taglines and click **Save Configurations**.
